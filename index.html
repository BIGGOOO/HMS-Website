FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS 10

Questions you should
ask before choosing an HMS.

Every deployment is different. These are the practical questions that usually shape a useful first conversation.

Can Seha One be customized for our hospital or clinic?+

Yes. The implementation begins by mapping your facilities, roles, workflows, forms, service lines, and integration needs. The right scope is determined during discovery.

Can it be deployed on-premise or in a private cloud?+

Yes. Deployment planning can consider cloud, private-cloud, and on-premise options alongside data-residency, security, and operating requirements.

Can it support multiple hospitals or facilities?+

CARE includes organisation, facility, and location models that can be configured for multi-facility operating environments. We validate the target model during solution design.

Can existing data and integrations be brought across?+

Data migration and integration feasibility are assessed before a pilot. The practical approach depends on the source systems, data quality, required interfaces, and governance rules.

Does the platform make us compliant with NPHIES or ZATCA FATOORA?+

No software alone creates compliance or regulatory approval. The platform can support implementation planning, but legal, technical, deployment, process, and operational controls must be assessed for your organisation.

What happens after the pilot?+

After pilot validation, the rollout can extend in phases with training, governance, support, integrations, migration, and measured adoption goals.
